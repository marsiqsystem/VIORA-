## Why jewellery is the go-to Rakhi gift

Raksha Bandhan is about showing your sister she's thought of — and jewellery does that better than almost any other gift. It's personal, it lasts, and every time she wears it she's reminded of you. Unlike chocolates or a card, a well-chosen **Rakhi jewellery** gift stays in her routine for months.

The trick is picking something she'll *actually* wear. That means matching the piece to her style, not yours.

## What to gift, by her style

### If she loves dressing up (weddings, parties, festive)
Go for a **stone necklace set** or a **bridal-style jewellery set** — a necklace with matching earrings makes a complete, occasion-ready look in one gift. These photograph beautifully and get worn to every function.

### If she keeps it simple (college, office, daily wear)
Pick a **delicate necklace** or a pair of everyday **earrings for women** — small studs, light drops or hoops. Something she can put on without thinking, that goes with kurtis, sarees and Western outfits alike.

### If she likes making a statement
A **long necklace**, layered chains, or bold chandbali-style earrings. Trend-forward pieces she'd love but might not buy for herself — which is exactly what makes them a great gift.

## How to choose so it doesn't look cheap

Good **artificial jewellery** and bad artificial jewellery cost about the same — the difference is in the finishing. Before you buy, check the listing for:

1. **The metal base** — quality fashion jewellery is made from **brass**, not thin light alloy.
2. **The finish** — a proper **rhodium plating** gives a bright, long-lasting shine. Viora Jewel pieces are heavy-polished and, with basic care, keep their shine for **1.5–2 years**.
3. **The stones** — original glass stones reflect light and sit securely; painted or loose stones are a red flag.
4. **A visible exchange policy** — a brand that offers a 48-hour exchange on damaged pieces is confident in its quality control.

If a listing says nothing specific ("premium quality jewellery"), be cautious. A brand that describes its brass base, rhodium finish and glass stones is a brand taking itself seriously.

## Budget guide

- **Under ₹500** — a single statement piece: earrings, or a delicate pendant.
- **₹500–₹900** — a complete necklace + earring set, the sweet spot for Rakhi.
- **₹900+** — a fuller set or a Rakhi gift combo, ideal if you want the gift to feel special.

You don't need to overspend for it to feel thoughtful. A well-chosen ₹699 set she wears 30 times beats a ₹2,000 piece she wears once.

## Caring for it so it lasts

Share these simple steps with her so the piece stays bright:

- Wipe it with a **soft, dry cloth** after every use.
- Keep it away from **perfume, alcohol-based products, water, sweat and harsh chemicals** — these shorten the life of the polish.
- **Store it dry**, in an airtight pouch or box, when not in use.

With this routine, a heavy-polished, rhodium-finished piece keeps its shine for **1.5–2 years**.

## When to order for Rakhi

This is where most people slip up. To be safe:

- **Standard delivery across India:** order **5–7 days** before Raksha Bandhan.
- **Remote pin codes:** give it **7–10 days**.
- Buying close to the date? Choose a brand that ships fast and sends a tracking link — and order the moment you've decided, not the night before.
