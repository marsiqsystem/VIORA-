## Caring for festive jewellery

Festive evenings mean perfume, sweat and late nights. To keep the shine:

- Put jewellery on **after** perfume and hairspray, not before.
- Wipe each piece with a **soft, dry cloth** before you put it away.
- **Store it dry**, in its own pouch or box.

With this routine, the rhodium finish typically lasts 1.5–2 years. More tips in our [care guide](/journal/how-to-care-for-fashion-jewellery-india).
